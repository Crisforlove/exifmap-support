Getting started

Tap Explore a sample journey to try ExifMap without importing your own photos. To use your own collection, choose Choose an album or Open a folder. Photos need existing GPS metadata to appear on the map.

Why are some photos missing from the map?

Some images have no GPS information, or location data was removed during editing or sharing. ExifMap lists photos without locations but does not infer a location from the image or write GPS information back to the original.

My album or folder will not load

Check Photos permissions in Settings, confirm the selected files are still available, and allow time for iCloud or your file provider to download remote assets. If a folder has moved or access changed, choose it again. Importing a large collection may take longer.

Live Photos

Compatible Live Photos imported from the Photos library can be played in the photo viewer. A JPEG or HEIC file stored alongside a MOV in a folder is not currently recognized as a Live Photo pair.

Directions

Open a located photo and tap Directions. ExifMap opens its recorded coordinate in Apple Maps. The coordinate is a photo capture point and may not be a business entrance. Apple Maps needs to be available for this action.

Sharing and local records

The share feature creates a static image, not an interactive map link. The most recent workspace is stored locally and references source photos; it does not back up your photo library.
