Privacy Policy

How ExifMap handles your photos and information.

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Developer: Crisforlove

Contact: Alpstar@proton.me

Photos and location information

ExifMap lets you choose a photo album or folder to view photos and their recorded capture locations. It reads information such as photo identifiers or file references, capture dates, GPS coordinates, and available camera metadata. Photo metadata and thumbnails are processed on your device. ExifMap does not upload your photo library or imported originals to a developer-operated server and does not modify your original photos.

ExifMap does not require an account. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Local storage

ExifMap saves your most recent workspace in the app's local storage. This may include photo or file references, metadata, selected dates, display preferences, and a folder-access bookmark. This workspace references its source photos and is not a backup of your originals. Exports and temporary files may remain in the app's local storage until cleaned up by the app or operating system.

Device backups and cloud storage are controlled by your device, Apple, and any file provider you choose. ExifMap does not provide a developer-operated workspace synchronization service.

Apple and other services

Maps and map snapshots use Apple MapKit services. Apple may process network requests and map-region information under its own privacy terms. Photos stored in iCloud and files stored with a cloud provider may be downloaded through those services when you access them. See Apple's privacy information.

When you select Directions, ExifMap passes the photo's destination coordinate and a destination label that can include its filename to Apple Maps. Route planning and any current-location permission are handled by Apple Maps, not by ExifMap's photo-location handoff.

Sharing

Sharing is initiated by you through the system share sheet. A photo card can include selected photos, their map locations, capture dates, and text you enter. Review the image before sharing, especially if it reveals a private location. The receiving app or service handles the content under its own terms and privacy policy.

Your choices

You can manage ExifMap's Photos access in device Settings and choose which folder to open. Removing permission may prevent ExifMap from loading referenced photos. Deleting the app removes its local app data through the operating system; original Photos-library assets and source files are not deleted by ExifMap. Copies you have shared or saved elsewhere and any system backups are managed separately.

Email support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to include, such as your email address, device details, and message. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot reported issues. Our email provider also processes the message to deliver and store email. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and related follow-up.

You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing Alpstar@proton.me. Do not send private photographs or precise locations unless you intend to share them for support.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no developer-added analytics, advertising, forms, cookies, or local-storage scripts. GitHub processes technical information, including visitors' IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Visiting an external link or contacting us by email is subject to the relevant service's privacy practices.

Contact and changes

Contact Alpstar@proton.me with questions about this policy or requests concerning information you have sent to support. This policy may be updated when the app's practices change; the effective date above identifies the current version.
